# Community expectations

English is preferred for cross-project collaboration; Chinese is welcome. Be specific about evidence, respect different experience levels, and separate technical disagreement from personal attacks.

No harassment, identity-based abuse, spam, fabricated endorsements or deceptive participation. Do not publish private customer evidence, signing keys, addresses or private contact details. Do not impersonate independent buyers, sellers, reviewers or operators to inflate interest or reputation.

Maintainers may moderate off-topic or abusive public participation and will explain ordinary scope decisions. Report a concern to the repository owner through an available GitHub channel; a private reporting endpoint has not yet been configured. Avoid sharing sensitive details in a public report.
